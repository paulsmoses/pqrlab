Srijana Shrestha, PhD Candidate, presents her work at the Texas Power & Energy Conference

Mar 2, 2026 · Dr. Paul Moses

PhD candidate Srijana Shrestha presented her paper, “Control of a Motor-Dynamometer Test Bench for HVAC Load Emulation in Grid Flexibility Research,” at the IEEE Texas Power and Energy Conference (TPEC) in College Town, TX. Her research addresses a critical gap in grid modernization: understanding how variable-speed HVAC systems—used for fast frequency regulation—affect long-term electric machine reliability. She developed a novel, low-cost motor-dynamometer test platform that emulates HVAC dynamics using two coupled induction machines with a coordinated control strategy.

This work was sponsored by U.S. Department of Energy, Office of Science, Office of Basic Energy Sciences, Establish Program to Stimulate Competitive Research (EPSCoR) under Award Number DESC0023498
