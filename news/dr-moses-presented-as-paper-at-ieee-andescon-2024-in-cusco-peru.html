Dr. Moses presented a paper at IEEE Andescon 2024 in Cusco, Peru

Sep 21, 2024 · Dr. Paul Moses

Dr. Moses had the great privilege of presenting a paper on behalf of his PhD student, Srijana Shrestha, titled "Application of a Squirrel Cage Induction Machine as a Dynamometer for Motor Heat-run Tests."

The paper was presented in Cusco, Peru on 13 Sept. 2024.
