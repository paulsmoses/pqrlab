Power system readiness for the 22nd century

Great breakthroughs begin with curiosity.

When Danish scientist Hans Christian Ørsted discovered that electric current creates a magnetic field, a simple observation transformed science and changed the world forever. Our research group explores the electro-physical phenomena that will shape the future of power and energy systems for the next century — from electromagnetic interactions and system resilience to advanced power technologies and grid modernization. The future of power systems begins with discovery.
